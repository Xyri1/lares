# Lares Logo Style Exploration

## Goal

Find an icon style for Lares before developing a final identity. The mark should
feel like an expressive companion without defining the appearance of a Lar.

## Constraints

- Icon mark only; no wordmark.
- Soft, organic, and friendly.
- Abstract rather than mascot-like.
- Recognizable at app- and tray-icon sizes.
- Monochrome-first and vector-friendly.
- Avoid robots, brains, chat bubbles, AI sparkles, and detailed character faces.

## Concepts

1. **Soft Loop:** one rounded continuous stroke suggesting a tilted face or
   embrace, with emotion flowing over time.
2. **Mood Seed:** an asymmetric bean-like form with minimal negative-space
   features, balancing warmth with abstraction.
3. **Breathing Bloom:** two or three soft lobes expanding around a center,
   suggesting breath and changing emotion.

## First Deliverable

One neutral comparison sheet showing one clean icon for each concept at equal
scale. Use dark marks on a warm off-white field with no gradients, mockups, or
decorative presentation. This sheet is exploratory and does not replace the
packaged Electron icon.

## Selection Test

Choose the direction that remains distinct at small size, feels alive without
becoming a mascot, and best represents a vessel for many user-selected
characters.
